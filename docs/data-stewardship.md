# Data stewardship

The corpus is maintained as a community resource. No individual contributor, administrator,
organisation or hosting provider owns the Gurbani content merely because they contributed to or
host the database.

Dhur Ki Bani is of the Guru and Akaal Purakh. It belongs to them and is available equally to
everyone, irrespective of caste, creed, religion or geography, to benefit from.

## Respectful use

Anyone downloading or redistributing content from this project is asked not to make unnecessary
or disrespectful alterations. The project itself never alters source Gurbani: every accepted
reading is traceable to its source snapshot, its evidence and the two people who approved it, and
every earlier reading remains available in the version history.

## How the software upholds this

- Source text is immutable and byte-exact; accepted text changes only by recorded two-person decisions.
- Provenance (source, snapshot hash, sync date, decision, approvers) is public through the API.
- Nothing is deleted from the research record; rollback adds a version.
- Contributors are credited by username only, and only for accepted work; rejected reports are never attributed publicly.
- The whole system, schema and tooling are open source so anyone can verify, reproduce or re-host the corpus.
